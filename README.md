# Hawwa - Merchant Analytics Platform

## Overview

Hawwa is an enterprise-grade e-commerce merchant analytics platform designed to help online merchants understand their business performance through unified metrics, analytics, dashboards, and predictive intelligence.

The platform simulates a real-world SaaS analytics ecosystem similar to modern e-commerce platforms.

## Business Objective

The goal of Hawwa is to provide merchants with:

- Sales intelligence
- Customer analytics
- Product performance insights
- Marketing analytics
- Inventory intelligence
- Merchant growth metrics
- Predictive analytics
- Data quality monitoring


## Key Capabilities

### Merchant Analytics

- Revenue tracking
- Order performance
- Store growth analysis
- Merchant benchmarking
