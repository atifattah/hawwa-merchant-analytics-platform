# Hawwa Business Requirements Document


## Product Vision

Hawwa provides merchants with a centralized analytics platform to monitor, analyze, and improve their e-commerce business performance.


## Target Users

### Merchant Owner

Needs:

- Revenue visibility
- Store performance
- Growth tracking


### Marketing Manager

Needs:

- Campaign performance
- Customer acquisition insights


### Operations Manager

Needs:

- Inventory monitoring
- Shipment tracking
- Return analysis


### Data Analyst

Needs:

- Trusted metrics
- Reusable datasets
- Business definitions


## Business Questions


### Sales

- What is my daily revenue?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- How is sales growth trending?


### Customers

- Who are my valuable customers?
- What percentage of customers repeat purchase?
- Which customers are likely to churn?


### Products

- Which products perform best?
- Which products have declining sales?


### Operations

- What is my delivery performance?
- What is my return rate?


### Merchant Health

- Is my store growing?
- Is my business at risk?
